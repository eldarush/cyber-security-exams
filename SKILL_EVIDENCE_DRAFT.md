`scripts/audit_skill_evidence.py` enumerates dates and IDs for the existing manual evidence map. Its counts only reflect manually mapped records, and the mapping groups related operations. It must not be treated as a complete binary labelling of every question. Mapped presence in A and B is neither independent of the map's retrospective construction nor sufficient to infer the probability of a particular variant. The seven pair sample is too small to calibrate the existing 1–100 scores.

Next audit steps: inspect every `csrf` and `hash` map entry against its complete question and options, distinguish direct mechanism from contextual mention and distractor-only mentions, then choose a transparent treatment for latest-A bonus. Recompute affected scores and focused selection together, verify all dependent explanations and history, and publish only after the scoring changes are justified and tested. Broad-topic score components should not be described as exact-operation recurrence.

## Follow-up on audited entries

Q004 and Q091 explicitly describe classic cookie-based CSRF among the statements, Q022 mentions CSRF as a statement and token as another, Q074 tests SameSite, and Q111 directly describes a CSRF scenario. Q076 concerns tricking the victim into disclosing a token; Q157 and Q180 focus on same-origin XSS and token access. Therefore P17 now uses the narrower `csrf-cross-site` mapping (Q004, Q022, Q074, Q091, Q111), while P05 retains the XSS/token family. For P15, Q067 and Q140 directly ask the fixed-message second-preimage definition; Q082/Q130 ask preimage, Q150 collision resistance, Q169 birthday calculation, and Q182 a toy collision. P15 now uses only Q067/Q140. P50 uses Q150/Q182 for collision rather than all hash-related questions. P06 uses Q057/Q116/Q133/Q143/Q163 for combinations of encryption and MAC, while P07's birthday calculation maps to Q169. The latter sets still cover related variants, not only identical operations; the scoring page now states this explicitly. Other skill families are not yet audited to this same level of specificity.

With those changes, the existing heuristic chooses P05 and P06 in the focused set in place of P17 and P15. P15 and P17 remain in the 60-question bank and can be practiced independently. A smaller or larger score is an editorial prioritization under unvalidated weights, not evidence of a calibrated likelihood. This observed change is model-sensitive and must not be interpreted as an experimentally established gain in predictive accuracy.
