`scripts/audit_skill_evidence.py` enumerates dates and IDs for the existing manual evidence map. Its counts only reflect manually mapped records, and the mapping groups related operations. It must not be treated as a complete binary labelling of every question. Mapped presence in A and B is neither independent of the map's retrospective construction nor sufficient to infer the probability of a particular variant. The seven pair sample is too small to calibrate the existing 1–100 scores.

The latter sets still cover related variants, not only identical operations; the scoring page now states this explicitly. Other skill families are not yet audited to this same level of specificity.

With those changes, the existing heuristic chooses P05 and P06 in the focused set in place of P17 and P15. P15 and P17 remain in the 60-question bank and can be practiced independently. A smaller or larger score is an editorial prioritization under unvalidated weights, not evidence of a calibrated likelihood. This observed change is model-sensitive and must not be interpreted as an experimentally established gain in predictive accuracy.
